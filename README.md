# Artificial Intelligence and DNA: Transforming Genomics Through Machine Learning

### Research Paper

An exploration of how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are transforming genomics, advancing DNA analysis, and creating new possibilities in biological research and medicine.

---

## Overview

This research explores the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and genetics, focusing on how machine learning techniques are being applied to understand, analyze, and interpret DNA.

By examining current developments and scientific research, the paper investigates how computational approaches are helping researchers identify genetic patterns, understand biological complexity, and advance genomic technologies.

The study aims to present these developments in an accessible yet scientifically grounded manner.

## Research Focus

The paper explores several key areas:

- **Artificial Intelligence in Genomics** — The role of AI in modern genetic research.
- **Machine Learning and DNA Analysis** — How machine learning models identify patterns and relationships in genetic data.
- **Genomic Applications** — The use of AI in understanding genetic variations and biological processes.
- **Scientific and Medical Implications** — The potential impact of AI-driven genomic research on healthcare and biotechnology.
- **Challenges and Future Directions** — Current limitations, ethical considerations, and opportunities for future development.

## Research Approach

This study is based on reviewing and examining scientific literature and published research in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and genomics.

The research draws on scientific publications and academic sources to explore existing methods, applications, developments, and challenges in the field.
